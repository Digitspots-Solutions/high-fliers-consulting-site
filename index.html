About Us

M Grace Securities Limited (trading as M Grace Capital Markets Co) is a registered financial services institution specializing in mortgage solutions and capital market services. Based in Alimosho, Lagos, we serve individuals, families, and corporate clients across Nigeria with integrity and expertise. Our team combines deep knowledge of the Nigerian financial landscape with a client-first approach, ensuring you receive personalized guidance at every step of your financial journey. Whether you're seeking your first home mortgage, refinancing options, or investment opportunities in the capital markets, M Grace Securities delivers solutions that work for you.
